Global Electricity Demand (2019–2026)

About the Data

Global electricity consumption by data centres, crypto, and AI is expected to more than double by 2026. Demand from these sectors could reach over 1000 TWh by 2026, up from around 460 TWh in 2022.

The chart above shows three forecast cases:

  • Low Case: Slower AI adoption, energy efficiency improvements, and reduced crypto mining lead to moderate growth.
  • Base Case: Expected growth based on current trends in data centre expansion, AI development, and cryptocurrency activity.
  • High Case: Aggressive growth in AI and digital services, with minimal regulatory or efficiency checks.

This increasing demand is driven by rapid expansion of data centres, cloud computing, cryptocurrency mining, and AI processing. Regions with growing tech infrastructure may see the highest electricity loads, potentially impacting power grids and sustainability efforts.

Source: IEA, IEEFA, projections based on 2019–2026 electricity consumption trends.
